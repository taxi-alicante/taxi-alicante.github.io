Available Options
💶 CARD, MOBILE PAYMENT OR CASH

The following conventional payment methods are accepted directly in the vehicle:

  • Cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express.
  • Mobile Payment: Apple Pay, Google Pay, Bizum.
  • Revolut: Revtag.
  • Cash: Local currency (Euros).
₿ PAY WITH BITCOIN (BTC)

In this taxi, you can pay with Bitcoin, using the payment method best suited to your journey.

1. Urban Trips

Payment is made at the end of the journey via Lightning Network, with virtually instant confirmation.

2. Intercity Trips and Airport Transfers

Lightning Network remains the preferred payment method. However, due to the longer duration of these journeys, Bitcoin On-Chain (Main Network) payments are also accepted at the start of the trip, based on an estimated fare calculated according to the official tariff.

Upon arrival, the final fare will be based on the taximeter. Any difference will be settled immediately using Lightning Network or cash.
